From your NAS to your next listen

A few steps.
Then press play.

  1. 01

    Install the app.

    Get Gumbo Music through TestFlight on your Apple device.

  2. 02

    Point it at your Synology.

    Sign in with your existing NAS account and choose your music folder. No extra media server to install.

  3. 03

    Your library, everywhere.

    Listen at home, stream away with remote access set up, or take your downloads offline.

A Synology NAS with DSM 7 or later and File Station is required. Listening away from home needs a reachable server connection, such as a configured HTTPS address or Tailscale.

A personal library should stay personal

Your library
stays yours.

Your originals stay on your NAS. Music streams directly to your devices, or plays from your local downloads. There’s no Gumbo music-storage service in between.

No separate Gumbo account.No upload to a cloud music library.No advertising or third-party analytics SDK.
What about iCloud and other services?+

Gumbo uses iCloud to sync profiles, playlists, favourites and listening data—not music files. Family sharing can also include NAS connection details.

If you choose genre suggestions, album and artist names are sent to Apple’s music catalogue. That optional lookup does not send your audio. Your existing NAS account is used to access your library.

TestFlight feedback and crash reports you share may be available to the developer. Full data-flow details are available in the app’s Privacy screen.
